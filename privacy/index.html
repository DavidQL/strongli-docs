Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Overview

Strongli is a mobile app for planning and tracking strength training activity. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how to contact us with privacy questions.

Information We Collect

Strongli may collect app usage information, product interaction data, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data. This information helps us understand how the app is working, identify bugs, improve reliability, and make the product better.

If you enter workout, training, or profile information into the app, that information is used to provide the app's features.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve Strongli.
  • Diagnose crashes, performance issues, and other app problems.
  • Understand which features are useful and how the app is used.
  • Respond to support or privacy requests.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate, analyze, or improve the app, and only as needed for those services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the app.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app, improve reliability, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Your Choices

You may contact us to ask questions, request access to information associated with you, or request deletion of information where applicable.

Children's Privacy

Strongli is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
